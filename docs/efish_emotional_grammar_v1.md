# efish Emotional Grammar V1

This is a public-safe product style configuration for the R29B2M-R4H hybrid
simulation. It is not private emotional data, a diagnosis system, a personality
profile, or evidence that an efish model has learned these behaviours.

The grammar maps an explicitly expressed or conservatively simulated affect and
dialogue act to voice, avoid flags, and a bounded response shape. It never adds
facts. The packet validator remains authoritative for exact anchor grounding and
for rejecting prompt injection, secret references, unknown labels, extra fields,
and oversized payloads.

The initial rule families cover tired, frustrated, relieved, sad, excited,
embarrassed, uncertain, reflective, playful, guarded, and ordinary conversation.
Their shared policy is to keep answers short, avoid customer-service and therapy
language, avoid unsolicited problem solving, and ask at most one necessary
question.

`owner_review_completed` remains `false`. The generated review pack under the
ignored R4H artifact root is the place for a later owner decision. These rules
must not be described as a complete representation of the user's personality.
