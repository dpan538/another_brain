After R25AM, the next allowed step is R25AN tokenizer and sampler readiness review only with fresh explicit approval. Future R25AO decoder micro-cycle training can only be considered after corpus, sampler, and tokenizer review, and it requires another fresh approval. Phase_4 remains blocked.
