R25AM may add a second reviewed repo-derived Chinese-personal split to `training/llm_corpus/`, but it does not authorize any tokenizer dry-run or decoder training.

After R25AM, the next allowed step is R25AN tokenizer readiness review only with fresh explicit approval.
