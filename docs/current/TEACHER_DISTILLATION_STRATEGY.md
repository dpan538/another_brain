# Teacher Distillation Strategy

Teacher models may be useful as acceleration references, but R27A does not call any teacher, call Doubao, automate desktop apps, or ingest teacher outputs as training data.

Teacher output is candidate comparison evidence, not truth. It cannot replace the user-answer corpus, cannot become a runtime dependency, and cannot be copied into final answers. It may later support planning, contrastive review, or preference-pair design only after explicit approval and review.

Any later teacher output must be user-supplied or produced by an explicitly approved local process, provenance-labeled, no-CoT, no-private-data, reviewed, transformed, and kept out of public commits unless a later policy explicitly allows a derived artifact.
