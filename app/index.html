efish other

this is efish other, an other.

about

efish other is a person, a memory, an efish and a dialog box.

它是人，是记忆，是鳄鱼，是对话框。

our small transformer + deepseek + an efish’s knowledge base + tuned by hand.

what it is

A web app that only talks: type, write, chat. It answers as one person’s other, in Chinese or English, never in more than two sentences. No accounts, no tools, no assistant features.

what you see

  1. a tree — person — 是人。
  2. a lock marked thirty — memory — 是记忆。锁在这台设备里，三十天。
  3. a cloud over two doors — 是鳄鱼。家在湖边。
  4. a loop: type, write, chat — 是对话框。可以打字。

The loop becomes the chat window. The keyboard is drawn by hand and carries its own pinyin input method.

what happens to what you type

It is sent through this site’s one server route to a language-model provider to be answered. Conversations are kept only on your device, for thirty days.

who made it

Dai Pan / 潘岱 is a visual artist, designer, photographer, printmaker and writer based in Brisbane, who moved from fine art into computer science and NLP research. efish other is his other. See also his portfolio daipan.art, the poems of Three Worlds at daipan.ink, and IN_PRAISE_OF_TIME.
