efish other is a person, a memory, an efish and a dialog box.

our small transformer
+ deepseek

What it is
  A web app (PWA) that only talks. No accounts, no tools, no assistant features.
  It answers as one person's other, in Chinese or English, never in more than two sentences.

What you see
  A drawing that moves when you scroll, in four acts made of the same lines:
    a tree            person       是人。
    a lock, "30"      memory       是记忆。锁在这台设备里，三十天。
    a cloud, two doors             是鳄鱼。家在湖边。
    a loop            type, write, chat      是对话框。可以打字。
  The loop becomes the chat window. The keyboard is drawn by hand and has its own pinyin
  input method; the 鳄 key opens a board of ready-made questions.

What happens to what you type
  It is sent through this site's one server route to a language-model provider to be answered.
  Your conversations are kept only on your device, for thirty days.

This site was rebuilt in September 2026. Descriptions of efishother.com as a "static q4
browser model demo", a "Dashboard" or a "Preview Engineering Candidate" describe the previous
site, which is archived and no longer served.
